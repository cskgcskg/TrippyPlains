50 lessons on the BEGINNER field

25 ANGLE lessons take you from starting the motors to a full course. 25 ACRO lessons, the mode real FPV pilots fly, take you to your first roll, first flip and a split-S. Helpers step back as you improve, and every card shows the exact stick to use.

Physics tuned by a real pilot

Tuned against a real 5-inch freestyle quad: no floaty hang at the top of a climb; it drops the moment you cut throttle. Fine-tune fall speed, apex hang, freefall drag and rotor brake with sliders, and reset to defaults in one click.

A real FPV view

120° camera like a modern digital FPV system, with the tips of your props in view. Four times of day, from dawn to a night under the Milky Way.

FAQ

Good to know

Does Trippy Plains run on Mac?

Yes. The Steam version runs on Windows and macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel, notarized by Apple). It is also on the Mac App Store.

Is it on iPhone?

Yes, on the App Store. On iPhone you fly with on-screen touch sticks; on a Mac you can use a keyboard, a gamepad or your radio.

Which controllers work?

FPV radio transmitters in USB joystick mode (including DJI remotes), Xbox, PlayStation and Switch Pro controllers, and keyboard. A calibration wizard maps any stick layout.

Do I need a powerful PC?

No. Trippy Plains is built to run smoothly on laptops, with quality modes for weaker hardware.

Is there multiplayer?

Yes, as a preview: online rooms through Steam, up to 8 pilots per room. Everyone in a room needs the same game version.

Is it good for learning FPV?

Yes. Start in angle mode, which levels itself, then switch to acro, the real FPV mode, when you are ready. The physics are tuned against a real 5-inch quad.

Who makes it?

A solo developer and FPV pilot, building with AI tools.
