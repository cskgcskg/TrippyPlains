Three new maps. A huge London update.

Fly a giant chess set, an ancient city and the desert, ride the Tube, and find cave paintings in a sea cave.

New map: CHECKMATE

A giant marble chess set on a terrace above the sea. Fly through the rook gates, the knight's mouth and the queen's crown while the pieces replay five famous games, one move every 8 seconds.

New map: PERGE

The ruins of an ancient city near Antalya, at real scale. Shoot the gap between the round gate towers, slalom the columns of the main street, and fly the baths, the stadium and the theatre.

Cave paintings in DRACH CAVES

22 painted panels and a 16 m painted gallery in the sea cave: horses, bison, deer, a mammoth, hand stencils and hunters. Our own Stone Age-style art, not copied from any real cave.

New map: DUNES

Desert dunes up to 150 m high. An oasis with date palms, a 5 m slot between two rock towers, an arch, and an oil rig whose drilling tower you can fly through.

LONDON, much bigger

A new start in St James's Park, Waterloo station with trains over the viaduct, St Paul's, Tube stations with escalators and running trains, Tower Bridge, the palace area, and 180 lost teddies to find.

MINI GAMES

48 cards on the start screen and in TAB: collect, race, smash, build, watch and explore. Each shows what to do and your progress, and PLAY takes you straight there.

A giant gaming PC on FRACTAL

80 times real size. Fly in through a front fan, over the graphics card and out of the back. Its monitor shows the game's own start screen.

Everywhere

FoV 83 by default for a more natural view (change it in TAB → Camera). A new factory FEEL for every drone. AUTO GRAB in CLAWTOWN. Confetti on every pickup.

v31 is the Steam version (Windows & Mac). The App Store version is separate and older, without these maps for now.

Why Trippy Plains

Built for real pilots.
Runs on what you have.

Whether you have never touched a radio or you fly acro every day, Trippy Plains meets you where you are.

🪂

Physics tuned by a real pilot

Tuned against a real 5-inch freestyle quad. Three FEEL sliders change how each drone flies (the middle is the factory feel), and you can type in your own rates.

🎥

A natural FPV view

FoV 83 by default, about 115° from side to side on a 16:9 screen. Change it in TAB → Camera, tilt the camera for each drone, and fly at dawn, noon, dusk or night.

🎮

Your radio or a gamepad

FPV radios in USB joystick mode, gamepads or keyboard. Calibrate the sticks, then put ARM, RESET, NEXT MAP and more on any button. The menus work on a controller.

💻

Runs on a laptop

Quality modes for modest hardware, and Q switches between fast and full quality. No gaming PC required.

🎯

48 mini games

Collect, race, smash, build, watch and explore. Each card shows what to do, the keys and your progress.

🏁

Races and gate tracks

Speedway races with 10 levels against AI rivals, 4 gate tracks each on FIELD, BANDO and THE YARD, and your best run comes back as a ghost to beat.

🌐

Fly together (preview)

Online rooms through Steam, up to 8 pilots per room. Join the same room name, or invite a Steam friend.

📸

Photo mode and clips

Freeze the moment for a clean screenshot. On Steam, press C to record up to 60 seconds of your flight with sound.

FAQ

Good to know

Does Trippy Plains run on Mac?

Yes. The Steam version runs on Windows and macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel, notarized by Apple). An older version is also on the App Store.

Is it on iPhone?

Yes, on the App Store. It is a separate, older version: it does not have the v31 maps yet. On iPhone you fly with on-screen touch sticks.

Which controllers work?

FPV radios in USB joystick mode, gamepads and keyboard. Calibrate the sticks in the game, then put ARM, RESET, NEXT MAP and more on any button.

Do I need a powerful PC?

No. Trippy Plains is built to run smoothly on laptops, with quality modes for weaker hardware. Q switches between fast and full quality.

Is there multiplayer?

Yes, as a preview in the Steam version: online rooms through Steam, up to 8 pilots per room. Everyone in a room needs the same game version.

Is it good for learning FPV?

Yes. The BEGINNER field has 50 step-by-step lessons: 25 in ANGLE mode, which levels itself, and 25 in ACRO, the real FPV mode, up to your first flip and a split-S. The physics are tuned against a real 5-inch quad.

Why does the camera say FoV 83?

The game measures FoV from top to bottom of the screen. 83 is about 115° from side to side on a 16:9 screen. You can change it in TAB → Camera.

Who makes it?

A solo developer and FPV pilot, building with AI tools.
