Why Trippy Plains

Built for real pilots. Runs on what you have.

Whether you have never touched a radio or you fly acro every day, Trippy Plains meets you where you are.

🪂

Physics tuned by a real pilot

Tuned against a real 5-inch freestyle quad. FEEL sliders change how each drone flies, and you can type in your own rates.

🎥

A natural FPV view

Set the field of view in TAB → Camera, tilt the camera for each drone, and fly at dawn, noon, dusk or night.

🎮

Your radio or a gamepad

FPV radios in USB joystick mode, gamepads or keyboard. Calibrate the sticks, then put ARM, RESET, NEXT MAP and more on any button. The menus work on a controller.

💻

Runs on a laptop

Quality modes for modest hardware, and Q switches between fast and full quality. No gaming PC required.

🏁

Races and gate tracks

Speedway races against AI rivals, gate tracks on FIELD, BANDO and THE YARD, and your best run comes back as a ghost to beat.

🌐

Fly together (preview)

Online rooms through Steam, up to 8 pilots per room. Join the same room name, or invite a Steam friend.

📸

Photo mode and clips

Freeze the moment for a clean screenshot. On Steam, press C to record up to 60 seconds of your flight with sound.

🎵

Original music

Original tracks, from psytrance to lo-fi, played live by the game. Music stays off until you turn it on.

Flight modes

Your pace. Your rules.

Switch modes at any time with a single key. Each mode has 25 step-by-step lessons on the BEGINNER field.

Angle

Self-levelling. The drone levels itself when you let go of the sticks. Pick it up in minutes and explore every map.

Acro

Full manual. The drone keeps your tilt, so you can flip and roll. The real FPV experience, and muscle memory for the real thing.

FAQ

Good to know

Does Trippy Plains run on Mac?

Yes. The Steam version runs on Windows and macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel, notarized by Apple). It is also on the App Store.

Is it on iPhone?

Yes, on the App Store. The App Store version is updated separately, so it can be behind the Steam version. On iPhone you fly with on-screen touch sticks.

Which controllers work?

FPV radios in USB joystick mode, gamepads and keyboard. Calibrate the sticks in the game, then put ARM, RESET, NEXT MAP and more on any button.

Do I need a powerful PC?

No. Trippy Plains is built to run smoothly on laptops, with quality modes for weaker hardware. Q switches between fast and full quality.

Is there multiplayer?

Yes, as a preview in the Steam version: online rooms through Steam, up to 8 pilots per room. Everyone in a room needs the same game version.

Is it good for learning FPV?

Yes. The BEGINNER field has 50 step-by-step lessons: 25 in ANGLE mode, which levels itself, and 25 in ACRO, the real FPV mode, up to your first flip and a split-S. The physics are tuned against a real 5-inch quad.

Can I try it for free?

Yes. The free demo runs in your browser at trippyplains.com/play: one map, CHECKMATE, with no install and no sign-up. Keyboard works in every browser; game pads and RC radios work in Chrome and Edge. In Safari, controller support is patchy.

Who makes it?

A solo developer and FPV pilot, building with AI tools.
