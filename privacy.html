Privacy Policy

Trippy Plains — FPV Freestyle Simulator  ·  Last updated: 11 November 2026

Overview

Trippy Plains is an FPV drone game. The game itself does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal data: there are no accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics and no advertising networks in the game. This website, trippyplains.com, uses Google Analytics only if you agree to it (see "This website" below).

What data the game stores

Trippy Plains saves a small amount of data locally on your device only, never on a server. This includes:

— Your in-game settings (graphics quality, controller calibration, camera angle, flight tuning, sound)
— Your best lap times on race maps
— Your preferred drone craft

This data exists only on your computer. Uninstalling the game removes it. We cannot access it and it is never sent anywhere.

The free browser demo

The free demo at trippyplains.com/play saves your settings (for example volume and controller setup) in your browser's local storage, on your device only. The demo sends no game data anywhere.

This website (trippyplains.com)

When you first visit, a small banner asks whether we may use Google Analytics cookies. Until you choose, and if you click Decline, Google Analytics is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

If you click Accept, the site loads Google Analytics through Google Tag Manager. It sets cookies and sends Google the pages you visit, the site or link you came from, your browser and device type, your approximate location (country or city, worked out from your IP address) and clicks on links such as the Steam buttons. We use this only to count visits and to see which posts and ads bring people to the site and the free demo. If we run Google or YouTube ads, the same data helps us measure them. We do not sell it, and we cannot see who you are.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. Google Analytics keeps it for at most 14 months.

Your choice is saved in your browser. You can change it at any time:

Steam

Trippy Plains is distributed through Steam. Steam is a platform operated by Valve Corporation. When you purchase and play games on Steam, Valve may collect certain data (your Steam account information, purchase history, playtime) in accordance with their own privacy policy. We do not receive any personally identifiable information from Steam beyond what is necessary to verify ownership of the game.

Steam Cloud saves (if enabled) store your settings on Valve's servers, subject to Steam's Privacy Policy.

Steam Achievements and Leaderboards store your in-game progress on Valve's servers. This is standard Steam platform functionality.

Fly together (preview)

In the Steam version, if you join a room in Fly together, your pilot name and your drone's position are shared with the other pilots in that room through Steam while you fly. We do not store them.

Steam Input / Gamepads

Trippy Plains supports gamepads and joysticks. Controller input is processed locally on your device to operate the in-game drone. No controller data is transmitted externally.

Children

Trippy Plains contains no age-restricted content and is suitable for all ages. The game does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page.
